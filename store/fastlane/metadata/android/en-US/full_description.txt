Every sound has a shape. Soundframe turns your voice, music, laughter, rain or any sound into unique generative art — a sound wave artwork no one else can make.

Record up to 10 seconds or import an audio file. Soundframe listens to the real character of your sound — loudness, pitch brightness, rhythm, texture and dynamics — and sculpts it into a living composition. The same sound always creates the same artwork, so every piece is truly yours.

★ FIVE GENERATIVE ART STYLES
• Aura — luminous ribbons that flow with the energy of your sound
• Bloom — an abstract flower whose petals are shaped by your sound's spectrum
• Particles — thousands of particles drifting like a constellation
• Topography — your sound as a landscape of glowing contour lines
• Echo — ripples of sound radiating outward like waves on water

Each style is a different generative algorithm, not a filter. A heartbeat, a whisper and a song look completely different.

★ MORE THAN AN AUDIO VISUALIZER
A classic audio visualizer draws a waveform. Soundframe analyses your audio on the device and composes art from it: bass gives weight and curves, high notes add fine detail, rhythm creates repetition, and loud moments glow brighter. It's sound visualization designed to be framed, shared and kept.

★ MAKE IT YOURS
• Seven curated colour palettes: Aurora, Solar Flare, Deep Ocean, Rose Quartz, Electric Violet, Nocturne and Monochrome
• Adjust intensity and complexity

★ YOUR PERSONAL SOUND GALLERY
Keep every creation in a beautiful gallery. Mark favourites, replay the original recording and revisit the sound signature behind each artwork.
