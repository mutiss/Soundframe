Privacy policy

Your sounds never leave your device.

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · App: SOUNDFRAME (com.mutissx.soundframe) for Android

In short

  • SOUNDFRAME analyses sound only on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • No account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • The developer does not collect, receive or share any personal data.
  • You can delete your recordings and artworks at any time.

1. Who is responsible

SOUNDFRAME is developed and published by Carlos Blaya García (“we”). For any question about this policy, contact carlosblayagarcia@gmail.com.

2. What the app does with your data

Microphone audio

When you tap record, SOUNDFRAME uses the microphone to capture up to 10 seconds of audio. The recording is analysed on your device to extract acoustic measurements (such as loudness, pitch brightness and rhythm) that shape your artwork. The microphone is used only while you are actively recording, and the permission is requested only when you first start a recording.

Imported audio files

If you choose an audio file with the system file picker, SOUNDFRAME reads it on your device to analyse it. The app only receives access to the specific file you select.

Artworks and recordings you save

Saved artworks, their settings, thumbnails and (if the “Keep original recordings” setting is on) the original recording are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Other apps cannot access them.

Preferences

Settings such as “Reduce motion” are stored locally on your device.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not transmit audio, artworks or any other data to our servers or anyone else’s. The app works fully offline and contains no networking, analytics, crash-reporting or advertising code.
  • We do not create user accounts or ask for your name, email, contacts, location or any identifier.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.
  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are disabled, so your recordings are not copied to cloud backups by the app.

4. When data leaves your device — only by your choice

Data leaves your device only when you explicitly decide so:

  • Share: when you share an exported image or video, Android’s share sheet sends that file to the app you pick (for example a messaging or social app). From then on, that app’s own privacy policy applies.
  • Save to device: exports you save are written to your device’s Pictures/Soundframe or Movies/Soundframe folder, where other apps with media access may see them.

5. Permissions

Record audio (RECORD_AUDIO) is the only permission SOUNDFRAME requests. You can deny it and still import audio files, and you can revoke it at any time in Android Settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete a single artwork (which also removes its recording and thumbnail), delete everything from Settings → Delete all creations, turn off “Keep original recordings”, or uninstall the app, which removes all of its data. Temporary files used during creation and export are cleared automatically.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect or process your personal data on any server, we hold no data about you to access, correct or delete. All data is under your control on your device. If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you retain your rights under the GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos).

8. Children

SOUNDFRAME does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, in-app communication or account features.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
